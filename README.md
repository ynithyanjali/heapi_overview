#Heapi
It's an application that i'm building to help people figure out what to do when someone needs first aid while help is on the way. You choose what is happening in plain language and get short step by step guidance you can read under stress.

The first aid app is still in development so i would love to hear from people who want to feel more prepared as well as EMTs and first-aid instructors who would be willing to review an early version of this creation.

https://heapi.app/#signup 

#Heapi Calm

It's a prototype which you will find in the website and you try it now!
It gives you one place to save the things you reach for when you feel overwhelmed, like a favorite song, a comfort show or a note to yourself without having spend your time by searching for it when you need it.
You can open them in ine tap and your lists stays on your device.

P.S : Currently trying to figure out ways to save the data you keep when you clear your cache or browser history.
